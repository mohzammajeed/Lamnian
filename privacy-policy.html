Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information Lamnian ("we", "us", the website at https://lamnian.vercel.app) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: if you sign in with Google or email, we receive your name, email address and profile photo (Google sign-in) and a unique user ID. Your name and photo appear on your public profile and posts. Your email address is never shown publicly.
  • Content you post: posts, photos, comments, likes, saved posts, listings (including any phone number, WhatsApp number and address you choose to add), and reports you send. This content is public, except reports and saved posts.
  • Technical data: browser type, device, approximate region and pages visited, collected by our hosting provider and by Google services described below.
  • Location: the weather and prayer times are for Lamnian. We do not ask for or track your device location.

2. How we use it

To run the website and your account, show posts and profiles, keep the community safe (reports, moderation), secure the service, and show advertising that keeps the site free.

3. Cookies and local storage

We use local storage on your device for language, dark mode, saved posts, offline copies of weather and posts, and to keep you signed in. Third parties such as Google use cookies as described below. You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings; some features may then stop working.

4. Advertising (Google AdSense)

  • We use Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, or visit aboutads.info. To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), we ask for your consent before using cookies for personalised ads.

5. Services we use

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore database) stores accounts and content. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel hosts the website and may log requests such as IP addresses for security and performance.
  • Open-Meteo and Aladhan provide weather and prayer-time data. Google Fonts serves the fonts.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers above, when required by law, or to protect the safety of our users and site. Content you post is visible to anyone.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your name and bio, or delete your posts, comments and entire account under Settings. Deleting your account removes your posts and profile.
  • Ask us to correct or remove information at Creatormms720@gmail.com.

8. Retention and security

We keep data until you delete it or your account. We rely on Firebase and Vercel security and on access rules that let each user change only their own data. No online service is perfectly secure.

9. Children

The site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

11. Contact

Lamnian Community, Creatormms720@gmail.com. See also our Terms of Use.
