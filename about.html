About Lamnian

Lamnian is a free, community-run website and installable web app for the people of Lamnian and the surrounding valley. It puts the information people look for every day in one place, in English and Urdu.

What you will find

  • Weather: live conditions, an hourly and 7-day forecast, air quality and practical advice, such as when to expect rain that can affect roads.
  • Prayer times: daily timings for the area, with a countdown to the next prayer.
  • Local news, events and notices: posts written by community members, including lost and found and announcements.
  • Business directory: shops, hotels, taxis, fruit and vegetables, repair services and more, with phone, WhatsApp and directions.
  • Places: visiting spots, mosques, schools, hospitals and public offices.

Who runs it

Lamnian is operated by Lamnian Community. Members post under their own name, and an administrator reviews reports and removes content that breaks our Community Guidelines, usually within hours.

Our sources

Weather data comes from Open-Meteo and prayer times from Aladhan. Posts and listings are written by community members and are not official statements. For emergencies and official notices, always check PMD, NDMA and your local authorities.
