Community Guidelines

Lamnian works because people trust it. Please help keep it useful and respectful.

Please do

  • Post real, useful local information: news, events, notices, honest business listings.
  • Give accurate details, such as dates, places and phone numbers, and name your source for official information.
  • Use your own photos or ones you have permission to share.
  • Disagree politely, especially on politics and civic issues.

Please do not post

  • Hate, harassment, threats, abuse or personal attacks, or content that targets people because of religion, sect, ethnicity, gender or origin.
  • False news, scams, fake jobs, fake donation appeals or misleading offers.
  • Violent, graphic, sexual or illegal content.
  • Other people's private information, such as phone numbers, addresses or photos, without their consent.
  • Copyrighted material you do not have the right to share.
  • Spam, repeated posts or ads unrelated to Lamnian.

Reporting and enforcement

Use ⋯ → Report on any post. The administrator reviews reports, usually within hours, and may remove content or accounts that break these rules. For serious or urgent concerns, email Creatormms720@gmail.com.
